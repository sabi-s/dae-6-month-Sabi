MD5 (unsuitable)                               0.0002 ms per hash

bcrypt, cost 12                              166.7849 ms per hash
Argon2id, 19 MiB, t=2, p=1                    14.2341 ms per hash
A purpose-built password hash is deliberately slow (and Argon2id also memory-hard), which is what
makes large-scale guessing against a stolen database expensive. Fast hashes are for files, not passwords.
